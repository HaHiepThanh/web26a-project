The hard question

“Why not just use Trello?”

Everyone asks, so here is the answer up front. Columns and cards are Trello's idea and we are not pretending otherwise. Trello is a good tool — it is just built for a different size of team than yours.

A tool that serves everyone has to keep every option open, and each option is one more thing on screen, one more decision before you start. Horizon Hub Harmony gives that up on purpose. Fewer moving parts, a board you can read at a glance, and work that finds its way onto it from the conversation you were already having.
