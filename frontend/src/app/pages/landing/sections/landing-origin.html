Our story

Three words. One board.

Three of us wanted a board with more in it and less in the way, so we built one. That took a fortnight. Naming it took longer.

Horizon noun
The far line a team keeps walking toward.
So the board is built around what comes next, not a monument to what is finished.
Hub noun
The one place everything meets.
So cards, chat, files and history sit on one screen, and nothing needs a second tab to make sense.
Harmony noun
Many people at once, and nobody stepping on anyone.
So everyone watches the same board change live, and roles decide who may change what.

Why it takes all three

  • A horizon without a hub is a goal nobody can see together.
  • A hub without harmony is one room with everybody talking over each other.
  • Harmony without a horizon is a happy team going nowhere.

Take any one away and the other two stop working. Together they are the whole brief: somewhere to go, one place to stand, and room for everyone to work at once.

Then someone noticed all three begin with the same letter. So do we.
